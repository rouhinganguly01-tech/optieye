End-to-End Managed Services

The Total Execution Plan.
Every Match. Every Season.

Technology is only half the solution. From the moment the league contract is signed, OptiEye assumes full operational control. We dispatch our hardware, deploy our native engineering teams, and manage the system for the entire duration of your league.

THE DEPLOYMENT PIPELINE

A standardized, broadcast-grade operational protocol executed by OptiEye professionals. Zero friction for league organizers.

PHASE 1

League Partnership & Audit

Following contract execution, our technical directors conduct a thorough digital and physical audit of all host stadiums. We calculate optimal tactical camera mounting points, evaluate local internet infrastructure, and establish secure transmission protocols to our centralized HQ.

PHASE 2

Hardware Dispatch

OptiEye mobilizes. We pack and dispatch our proprietary 5-camera optical rigs, customized edge-computing modules, and secure networking hardware from our central repository directly to the league's host cities, ensuring zero logistical burden on the federation.

PHASE 3

Pre-Tournament Rigging

Days before the opening match, our ground engineering teams physically install the camera arrays. Tactical wide-shots and 16-meter penalty line cameras are rigged perfectly parallel to the pitch, ensuring they do not interfere with standard broadcast production crews.

PHASE 4

Network Sync & Calibration

The cameras are hardwired into our edge modules. Our remote Video Operation Room (VOR) pings the stadium. Using proprietary OpenCV matrix algorithms, we synchronize the video frames across all 5 feeds to ensure absolute zero-latency playback for the officiating team.

PHASE 5

Live Matchday Operations

Throughout the league calendar, our IFAB-accredited VAR operators and Replay Technicians monitor the matches live from the centralized VOR. The system strictly adheres to FIFA VAR Lite parameters, initiating direct audio communication with the on-pitch referee only for clear and obvious errors.

PHASE 6

Archiving & Demobilization

Upon the final whistle of the league, all calibrated offside logs, synchronized multi-angle video files, and referee audio communications are securely encrypted and handed over to the federation. Our ground team safely strikes all hardware and returns to HQ.
