The OptiEye Story

Born from a deep passion for the game. Engineered to eradicate the officiating divide. We are building the future of football technology from the ground up.

Democratizing
Fair Play.

For decades, professional Video Assistant Referee (VAR) technology has been gatekept by massive capital expenditures. The top 1% of global football leagues enjoy absolute accuracy, while emerging, Tier-2, and regional leagues are left exposed to critical officiating errors.

OptiEye was founded to shatter this technical monopoly. We believe that match integrity should not be dictated by a federation's budget. By rethinking how video processing, camera synchronization, and remote officiating operate, we have created a broadcast-grade system accessible to the rest of the world.

VARaaS
VAR-AS-A-SERVICE

Indigenous Engineering.

We didn't just assemble off-the-shelf parts; we built the core engine from scratch. OptiEye VAR Lite is proudly developed in India, utilizing a highly optimized software stack to guarantee ultra-low latency processing even in demanding stadium environments.

OpenCV Vision

Powered by advanced computer vision algorithms to track, sync, and calibrate offside lines across up to 5 simultaneous broadcast feeds with pinpoint accuracy.

C++ & Python Core

A robust backend architecture that ensures less than 0.5s system latency. The software is engineered for relentless stability during 90+ minutes of high-stakes play.

Centralized VOR

By moving the Video Operation Room to a remote, centralized hub, we eliminate the need for heavy on-site server racks and mobile vans at the stadium.

A Global Standard,
Made in India.

Our vision extends far beyond domestic borders. While OptiEye is deeply rooted in India's growing football ecosystem, our 'Managed VAR Services' model is built to scale internationally.

By supplying our own proprietary technology, network infrastructure, and IFAB-trained officiating personnel, federations around the world can implement professional VAR without the paralyzing operational headaches.
